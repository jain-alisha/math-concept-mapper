Privacy & student data

Updated November 13, 2026.

You can build a map without an account. Use concept labels and explanations, rather than names or other identifying information, in student maps.

What Span stores

  • Without an account: map drafts and preferences are saved in this browser’s local storage. People using the same browser profile may be able to see them. Clear Span’s site data in your browser settings to remove local drafts and preferences.
  • With an account: Student accounts use a username and password without a personal email address. Supabase stores an internal, undeliverable login identifier; Span stores the username, a hash of the recovery key, age group (under 13 or 13+), and any adult-authorization reference. Email accounts still store an email address and optional display name. Cloud maps include titles, concepts, arrows, notes, dates, and activity or timeline assignments. Class records include membership, invite codes, and roster names and email addresses.
  • Explore: asking for related ideas sends concept labels and curriculum metadata to Span’s recommendation service. When an external AI provider is configured, the selected concept and candidate curriculum topics are sent to that provider. Do not enter personal information in concept labels. Prerequisite and next-step suggestions use the bundled curriculum graph in your browser. Maps do not need Explore to be created or saved.

Who can see a map

Signed-in users can access their own cloud maps. Teachers can view maps attached to activities in their own class, rather than a student’s personal maps or work for other classes. The demo currently allows signed-in users to switch into teacher mode; this must be restricted before real classroom use. Class invite codes should be shared only with intended participants.

A share link contains a map snapshot in its URL. Anyone who receives that link can read the snapshot and forward it. Removing a saved map does not recall links or exported PDFs already shared.

Use, retention, and deletion

Span uses map and class information to provide mapping, cloud saving, class views, and learning activities. This application does not include advertising or sell student information. Hosting, authentication, and any configured AI services process requests to run these features.

Cloud maps and starting checkpoints are automatically deleted after 12 months without edits. Export anything you want to keep. Delete individual maps in My Maps, or delete your account in Settings → Classes → Your data; account deletion removes its cloud maps, timelines, memberships and any classes you teach. Accounts otherwise remain until you delete them. A parent or school can contact Span to revoke an authorization and arrange deletion. Supabase’s primary database for this project is in North Virginia, US (us-east-1). Backups and provider logs follow the providers’ configured retention, and deleting an account cannot recall previously shared links or exports.

Children and school use

Under-13 username signup requires a valid authorization code. Span issues codes only after an administrator verifies parent or school authorization and records evidence; the code is independent of class enrollment. It does not enroll the student in a class. Do not use the adult/13+ email-account option for an under-13 child. COPPA places obligations on operators collecting children’s personal information; a school’s authority to consent is limited to the educational context. See the FTC’s COPPA guidance for schools.

Schools must review and approve tools that handle education records. A school relying on FERPA’s school-official exception must retain the required control over the use and maintenance of those records, including limits on reuse and disclosure. See the U.S. Department of Education guidance. This notice is not a school data-processing agreement or a claim of FERPA/COPPA certification.

Contact, access, and corrections

Contact the Span team through the project’s support page for privacy questions, teacher-access verification, or to arrange a private deletion or correction request. The support page is public: do not post student names, email addresses, map contents, passwords, or other private information. Request a private follow-up channel before sharing account details.
