Start with a topic. See where it leads.

Span turns a flat list of curriculum topics into a living map — the prerequisites, extensions, and connections between ideas that a list can't show. Built for teachers planning a sequence, students tracing why a topic matters, and curriculum designers spotting the gaps in between.

Three ways it helps

Map an entire curriculum

Browse a full grade-by-grade, unit-by-unit hierarchy and drag any topic straight onto the canvas.

See relationships, not just lists

Draw arrows between concepts to show prerequisites, extensions, and related ideas.

Discover what connects next

Long-press any node to get AI-suggested prerequisites, successors, and related topics.

For Teachers

See where students may be missing prerequisite connections.
