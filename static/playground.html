How to use:
  • Drag a concept from the sidebar or from the AI recommender onto the map.
  • Custom node: Enter any label below and add your own concept to the map.
  • To create a link: Double-click and drag from one node to another.
  • To add a note to a link: Double-click on the link line.
  • To get AI recommendations: Long-press (hold mouse) on a node.
  • To move a node: Drag it on the map.
  • Right-click a node or link to delete.
